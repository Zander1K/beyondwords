Style: clean, modern editorial illustration for teenage and adult readers, like the art in a well-designed non-fiction book or museum guide. Natural proportions and a calm, muted palette with one or two stronger colours. Clear shapes and confident outlines, light shading, no clutter. Not childish: no cartoon faces, big eyes, chibi proportions or toy-like rendering. Plain light background. One clear subject, centred, filling most of the frame, easy to recognise at a glance on a very small screen.

Rules: one picture only, never a comic strip or several panels. Do not write any text, letters, numbers, labels, captions or speech bubbles anywhere in the image, even if the description contains quoted words. No real people's faces. Keep it calm: nothing gory or upsetting, even if the description is; show tension through the scene, not through injury or fear.
